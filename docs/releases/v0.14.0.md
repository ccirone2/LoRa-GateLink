## Install notes

<!-- notes:start -->
- **Anomalies.**
  - `lbt_forced` with waits of 504 ms (house CMD, long soak) and 498 ms (gate STATUS, house power cut): both came right after the link had been cut, a 4 s outage in one case and the house's supply cut in the other. A board's own resends kept the air busy, so the frame went through the 0.13.7 wait cap, as designed.
    - In the long soak the gate got the command 47 ms later and pulsed once.
  - One USB line was lost (`usb_cut`) while the house's supply was being cut. Its UART tap carried the run.
- **Full-suite runs and pytest versions.**
  - Run `20261011-000339` used pytest 9.1.1 and cryptography 50.0.2 (the versions #92 moved to), in a separate environment before #92 merged.
  - Run `20261010-223458`, on pytest 8.3.3, also passed 65.
- **Runs lost and re-run.**
  - The first 0.14.0 runs, `20261010-203811` (full suite, 65 passed) and `20261010-205708` (60-min long soak, passed), were deleted along with a git worktree before they could be collected.
  - Main's firmware tree was unchanged, so they were re-run from main: `20261010-223458` and `20261010-230147`. #89's body has the originals.
- **History across power cuts.**
  - `test_gate_power_cut_keeps_history` (#93) is in the `-m power` run above.
  - Earlier, by hand: a supply cut of each board with 4 completed 60 s buckets; all came back identical.
  - 34 gate supply cuts timed around a history-sector erase all booted with `persist` true and a coherent history, and the next write worked (#93 has the details).
- **Not checked on the bench.**
  - D5 with a meter or a relay module: its level through a reset and the bootloader, and its timing.
  - Boot time with a full 512-record history log.
  - The 0.13.9 paths the bench can't trigger on demand (TODO.md): a radio fault mid-pulse, five remote writes in one pulse, and the switch flipped back between a CLOSE's ACK and the next STATUS.
